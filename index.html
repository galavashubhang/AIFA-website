FractoAI Workflow

Failure analysis is the process of identifying the root cause of a component's failure, which is traditionally manual, slow, and subjective[cite: 1]. AI-assisted methods bring automation, objectivity, and faster diagnosis[cite: 1].


1
Upload: Upload failure evidence, component context, material, and up to five images[cite: 1].
2
Inspect (Agent 1): Reads images and reports observed features without assigning a cause at this stage[cite: 1].
3
Investigate (Agent 2): Generates targeted questions adapted to the visual evidence[cite: 1].
4
Analyze (Agents 3 & 4): Fuses image data and answers, retrieves similar cases from a vector search over 90 sourced failures, ranks hypotheses, and recommends lab tests[cite: 1].
5
Report: Generates a 12-section engineering PDF ready to share[cite: 1].

Novelty & The Moat

  • Multimodal & General: Adapts to any component or material using up to five views[cite: 1].
  • Grounded Reasoning: Powered by retrieval over real, source-cited failures, reasoning from precedent rather than guesswork[cite: 1].
  • Proprietary Data Moat: Enriches a private, outcome-labelled failure database per plant that compounds and is hard to copy[cite: 1].
  • Expert Grounding: Built on real refinery cases from MRPL and expert grounding from day one[cite: 1].

Prototype Validation

Tested against five real, unpublished MRPL refinery failures run end-to-end from photos and context. FractoAI's top hypothesis was compared to each documented root-cause report[cite: 1].
